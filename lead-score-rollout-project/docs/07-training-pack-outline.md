# Training pack outline

## Representatives (90 min plus floor-walk)
- Purpose of the model and what it learned from
- Reading a score: band, reason codes, limits
- Hands-on: work 10 past leads, compare outcomes
- Override rules and reason codes
- Support channels, one-page card, 3-minute videos, quiz (80% to pass)

## Managers (2 h plus 30-min coaching clinic)
- All representative content plus the team dashboard
- Weekly 15-minute review: SLA, overrides, adoption
- Coaching representatives who ignore or over-trust scores
- Approving and auditing overrides; requesting rollback

## Score bands
Hot (top 10%): contact within 15 minutes. Warm (next 30%): same day. Cool (next 40%) and Cold (bottom 20%): normal cadence.

## Override policy
- Allowed with a reason code: existing customer or live deal, direct buying signal not in the CRM, duplicate or test lead, competitor, compliance block.
- Not allowed: personal impression of the lead or avoiding a difficult call.
- Expected override rate 5% to 25%. Below 3% signals blind trust; above 30% signals distrust.
